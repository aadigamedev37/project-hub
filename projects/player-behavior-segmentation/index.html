What 40,034 players reveal about how people play

This dashboard is an interactive exploration of online gaming behavior, identifying distinct player segments through unsupervised machine learning. It exists to help game designers and live-operations teams understand player pacing, engagement depth, and monetization paths.

Choosing K & Feature Signals

To determine the optimal number of player segments, we tested K=2 through K=8 using K-Means clustering. The results indicate that the data does not contain sharply separated, distinct clusters.

Meaningful Structure Check

Given the continuous nature of gameplay telemetry, players exist on a continuous behavioral spectrum. K=4 was selected as an actionable, pragmatic partition for game design and live-ops strategy.

Metric Value Reading
Silhouette Score 0.114 Low (< 0.25). Clusters overlap gracefully along continuous boundaries.
Davies-Bouldin 2.28 Centroids are relatively close compared to intra-cluster spread.
Calinski-Harabasz 6,842 Adequate variance explained, driven primarily by PlayTime and Level.

Feature Signal Analysis (η²)

Eta Squared (η²) measures the proportion of variance explained by the K=4 clustering. Clustering is dominated heavily by PlayTimeHours, PlayerLevel, and InGamePurchases, whereas Session count and Duration contribute lower effect sizes.

Method & limitations

This section describes the analytical pipeline, methodological assumptions, and limitations of applying K-Means to behavioral game telemetry.

Methodology

Features were standardized using StandardScaler to account for scale variance across unbounded continuous metrics (PlayTime: 0–30) and discrete scores (Achievements: 0–50). The model was fitted using Euclidean distance with k-means++ centroid initialization.

Known Limitations

1. Synthetic Nature: The dataset exhibits uniform feature distributions rather than the power-law (Pareto) curves typical of real-world Free-to-Play titles.

2. Continuous Boundaries: Because player metrics form a continuous density spectrum, segment boundaries should be interpreted as operational partitions rather than rigid demographic groups.
